Brake pad replacement cost

Brake pad replacement in the United States usually costs $150 to $450 per axle, with parts and labor included. Independent shops are often near $150 to $300, while dealers can reach $250 to $450. Adding new rotors raises the bill to roughly $300 to $800 per axle.

What changes the price

  • Whether you also replace the rotors, which adds about $40 to $200 each.
  • Car type: trucks, luxury and performance cars cost more.
  • Pad quality: basic, ceramic or OEM pads have different prices.
  • Shop type: dealers charge more than independent shops.
  • Local labor rates, which are often $90 to $200 per hour.
  • Extra work such as caliper service or a brake fluid flush.

How not to overpay

  • Ask whether the rotors really need replacing or can be left as they are.
  • Compare an independent shop with the dealer.
  • Ask for an itemized quote that lists parts and labor separately.
  • Ask about aftermarket pads from a known brand versus OEM pads.
  • Replace pads before they wear down to metal, because that damages the rotors and costs more.

FAQ

How much does it cost to replace brake pads on one axle?

Most US shops charge about $150 to $450 per axle for pads only, parts and labor included. Independent shops are often at the lower end and dealers at the higher end.

How much to replace brake pads and rotors?

Pads and rotors together usually cost about $300 to $800 per axle. Trucks, luxury and performance cars can cost more.

Do I need to replace front and rear brake pads at the same time?

No. Front pads usually wear faster, so many cars need them replaced more often than the rear ones. Each axle is priced separately.

How long does a brake pad replacement take?

A pad job typically takes about 1 to 1.5 hours per axle at a shop.
