REPORT / 02The overview

This is a 2018 Ford Escape SEL, a compact SUV with a 1.5L 4-cylinder gasoline engine, equipped with automatic transmission and 4-wheel drive.

REPORT / 03Reliability

The 2018 Ford Escape has a moderate reliability record with some reported issues in the 1.5L EcoBoost engine and transmission components; overall, it is average for its class and age.

REPORT / 04Risk

Potential risks include turbo engine reliability concerns, transmission issues, and higher maintenance costs associated with 4WD systems.

REPORT / 05Ownership cost

Medium — maintenance and repair costs are typical for a compact SUV with a turbocharged engine and 4WD system.

REPORT / 06Budget warning

Expect moderate ongoing costs due to turbocharged engine maintenance and potential 4WD system repairs.

REPORT / 07Who this car suits

Ideal for buyers seeking a compact 4WD SUV with moderate power who are willing to invest in thorough inspection and maintenance.

REPORT / 08Worth a visit?

Maybe — get proof first; ensure maintenance records and inspection reports are available before committing to a visit.

REPORT / 09Red flags
  1. Missing or incomplete maintenance records.
  2. Signs of transmission slipping or rough shifting.
  3. Unusual noises or performance issues from the turbo engine.
REPORT / 10Weak points
  1. 1.5L EcoBoost turbo engine reliability concerns.
  2. Automatic transmission durability under 4WD conditions.
  3. Potential higher maintenance costs for 4WD components.
REPORT / 11What to check
  1. Turbocharger condition and boost pressure consistency.
  2. Transmission operation and fluid condition.
  3. 4WD system engagement and differential function.
  4. Overall vehicle service history and any past repairs.
REPORT / 12Questions to ask the seller
  1. Has the turbocharger or transmission ever been repaired or replaced?
  2. Can you provide a complete maintenance and service history?
  3. Are there any outstanding recalls or unresolved issues?
  4. Has the 4WD system been regularly serviced and tested?
  5. Has the vehicle been involved in any accidents or had major repairs?
REPORT / 13Negotiation tips
  1. Use any known turbo or transmission issues as leverage for price reduction.
  2. Request discounts if maintenance records are incomplete or missing.
  3. Negotiate based on the cost of potential 4WD system repairs.
  4. Consider the vehicle's mileage and condition relative to asking price.
REPORT / 14Your next steps

1. Obtain a full service history and verify regular maintenance. 2. Have a qualified mechanic inspect the turbocharged engine and transmission thoroughly. 3. Check for any recalls or service bulletins related to the 2018 Escape. 4. Test drive to assess 4WD operation and transmission shifting quality.

Recall campaigns

17V803000AIR BAGS:SIDE/WINDOW

Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2018 Ford Escape vehicles. The side curtain air bags on these vehicles have a component that may detach during deployment of the inflatable curtain air bag.

18V104000SERVICE BRAKES, HYDRAULIC:FOUNDATION COMPONENTS:HOSES, LINES/PIPING, AND FITTINGS

Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2018 Ford Escape and Lincoln MKC vehicles. These vehicles may be equipped with incorrect front brake hoses that may chafe against other components, possibly resulting in a brake fluid leak.

22V413000POWER TRAIN:AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION:GEAR POSITION INDICATION (PRNDL)

Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2013-2019 Escape, 2013-2018 C-Max, 2013-2016 Fusion, 2013-2021 Transit Connect, and 2015-2018 Edge vehicles. The bushing that attaches the shifter cable to the transmission may degrade or detach.

26V011000EQUIPMENT:ELECTRICAL:ENGINE BLOCK HEATER

Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2013-2018 Focus, 2013-2019 Escape, and 2015-2016 MKC vehicles equipped with a 2.0L engine. The engine block heater may crack and develop a coolant leak, causing it to short circuit when the block heater is plugged in.
