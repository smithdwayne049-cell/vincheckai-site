Car dent repair cost

Fixing a single car dent in the United States usually costs about $75 to $600. Small door dings repaired with paintless dent repair run about $75 to $300, while larger or more complex dents cost about $250 to $600. Dents that need body filler and paint, or hail damage across a whole car, cost much more.

What changes the price

  • Dent size, depth and sharpness.
  • Whether the paint is cracked, which rules out paintless repair.
  • Panel material: aluminum costs more than steel.
  • Dents near a panel edge or crease are harder.
  • Number of dents on the same panel.
  • Local labor rates and shop type.

How not to overpay

  • Ask a paintless dent repair specialist first if the paint is not cracked.
  • Get a written quote that names the method, paintless or filler and paint.
  • Get a second quote from another shop before you agree.
  • Ask about a discount when fixing several dents in one visit.
  • Check your insurance deductible before using a claim for a small dent.

FAQ

How much does it cost to fix a dent in a car?

A single dent usually costs about $75 to $600 in the US. Small dings are at the low end, and bigger dents need more time and skill.

Is paintless dent repair cheaper?

Usually yes, if the paint is intact and the dent is in a reachable spot. It avoids body filler and repainting.

How much does hail damage repair cost?

Hail repair on a whole vehicle can run about $1,000 to $5,000 or more, depending on how many dents there are.

Can every dent be fixed without paint?

No. Cracked paint, very sharp creases and some edges need traditional bodywork and paint.
