Bumper repair cost

Bumper repair in the United States usually costs about $150 to $600 for the cover, with refinishing often $250 to $600 more. A painted bumper replacement runs about $800 to $2,500 for mainstream cars. Sensor and driver-assistance recalibration can add $300 to $800, and luxury or EV models can cost more.

What changes the price

  • Repair or replace, which depends on how badly the cover is cracked.
  • Paint color and blending into nearby panels.
  • Parking sensors, cameras and radar behind the bumper.
  • Car type: luxury, truck and EV models cost more.
  • Parts: OEM, aftermarket or used covers.
  • Shop type and labor rates.

How not to overpay

  • Ask if the bumper can be repaired instead of replaced.
  • Ask which sensors need recalibration and what it adds to the quote.
  • Ask for an itemized quote that lists parts and labor separately.
  • Get a second quote from another shop before you agree.
  • Compare OEM, aftermarket and used cover prices.

FAQ

How much does it cost to repair a bumper?

Most repairs cost about $150 to $600, and refinishing adds more. A full painted replacement costs about $800 to $2,500.

Is it cheaper to repair or replace a bumper?

Repair is cheaper for scuffs and light cracks. Replacement makes sense when the cover is badly cracked or the mounts are broken.

Do parking sensors raise bumper repair costs?

Yes. Sensors, cameras and radar may need repair or recalibration, which can add $300 to $800 or more.

How long does bumper repair take?

Simple repairs can often be done in a day. Replacement with paint often takes a few days.
