Where to find the VIN on a Toyota

Look at the bottom-left of the windshield on the driver side, or the sticker on the driver door jamb. You can also find the VIN on registration or title documents and insurance papers. Compare it with the VIN on the car.

What the 17 characters mean

For standard US-market passenger vehicles: 1–3 identify the manufacturer (WMI); 4–8 describe the vehicle; 9 is the check digit; 10 identifies the model year; 11 identifies the plant; 12–17 form the serial number. Rules can differ by market and manufacturer.
