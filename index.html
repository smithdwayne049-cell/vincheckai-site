What is a VIN?

A vehicle identification number (VIN) is a unique 17-character identifier used on modern vehicles. A VIN decoder turns that identifier into vehicle specifications such as model, engine and drivetrain.

Where can I find my VIN?

Look at the bottom of the windshield on the driver’s side, on the driver’s door jamb, or on the vehicle registration and insurance documents. In the app, scan it with your camera or type it in.

What does a Vin AI report include?

A full AI report brings together decoded specifications, an AI buy / caution / walk verdict and inspection score, red flags, things to inspect, seller questions and negotiation tips. The app also offers market value and mileage information, NHTSA safety recalls, owner complaints and crash ratings, and EPA fuel economy. Coverage and availability vary by vehicle and source.

Is a VIN check free?

Basic VIN decode is free in the app. A full AI report costs $3.99 as a one-time purchase, with no subscription needed.
