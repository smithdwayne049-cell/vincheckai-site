Water pump replacement cost

Water pump replacement in the United States usually costs about $250 to $1,400, parts and labor included. A pump that is easy to reach costs less, and a pump buried under other parts costs more. Electric pumps cost more than belt-driven ones.

Total, parts and labor$250 – $1,400
Part of the jobLowHigh
Total, parts and labor$250$1,400

Estimates for United States, November 2026. Real prices depend on the car, the region and the shop.

What changes the price

  • Labor: on some engines the radiator, hoses and pulleys must come off first.
  • Pump type: belt-driven pumps are cheaper than electric ones.
  • Car make and engine.
  • Whether the timing belt is replaced at the same time.
  • Coolant, thermostat and hoses that are often replaced with it.
  • Shop type and local labor rates.

How not to overpay

  • If the pump is driven by the timing belt, ask to replace both together to save labor.
  • Ask for an itemized quote that lists parts and labor separately.
  • Get a second quote from another shop before you agree.
  • Ask about the pump brand, since cheap pumps may wear out faster.
  • Ask whether a coolant flush is included or extra.

FAQ

How much does a water pump replacement cost?

In the US most jobs cost about $250 to $1,400 depending on the car. Labor is often the biggest part of the bill.

What are the signs of a failing water pump?

Common signs are a coolant leak, an engine that runs hot, or a whining noise from the front of the engine. Check the temperature gauge and stop driving if it climbs.

Should I replace the water pump with the timing belt?

If the water pump is driven by the timing belt, yes. The same labor covers both, so doing them together usually costs less than two separate jobs.

How long does a water pump last?

Many pumps last many years, but cheaper replacement pumps may wear out sooner, in some cases after about 30,000 miles.
