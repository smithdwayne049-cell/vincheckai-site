Headlight replacement cost

Headlight replacement in the United States costs about $30 to $1,500 depending on the type. A bulb swap by a shop can cost $30 to $300, a halogen assembly about $200 to $500, and a modern LED assembly about $750 to $1,500. Some luxury and matrix systems cost far more.

Estimates for United States, November 2026. Real prices depend on the car, the region and the shop.

What changes the price

  • Bulb or full sealed assembly.
  • Halogen, HID or LED lighting.
  • Car make: luxury and matrix headlights cost far more.
  • Whether the bumper has to come off for access.
  • OEM, aftermarket or used assembly.
  • Whether the headlights need aiming afterward.

How not to overpay

  • Check if only the bulb is bad before buying an assembly.
  • Ask if a used or aftermarket assembly is approved for your car.
  • Ask for an itemized quote that lists parts and labor separately.
  • Get a second quote from another shop before you agree.
  • Ask whether headlight aiming is included.

FAQ

How much does it cost to replace a headlight?

A bulb swap by a shop costs about $30 to $300. A full assembly costs about $200 to $500 for halogen and about $750 to $1,500 for LED.

Why are modern headlights so expensive?

Many cars use sealed LED units that cannot be opened, so the whole assembly must be replaced. Some luxury systems can cost thousands.

Can I replace a headlight bulb myself?

On many cars yes, and it saves labor. On some cars the bumper or other parts must come off, so a shop may be the better choice.

Do headlights need to be aimed after replacement?

Often yes, especially after replacing a full assembly, so the beam points the right way.
