Toyota Camry water pump replacement cost

Replacing the water pump on a Toyota Camry in the United States usually costs about $500 to $1,400, parts and labor included, based on estimates for several model years. Older Camrys with an easy-to-reach pump can come in lower, and the price depends on the year, engine and your area.

Estimates for United States, November 2026. Real prices depend on the car, the region and the shop.

What changes the price

  • Model year and engine, such as four-cylinder or V6.
  • Pump design: belt-driven or electric.
  • Local labor rates and shop type.
  • Whether coolant, thermostat or belts are replaced with the pump.
  • Dealer or independent shop.
  • Parts brand.

How not to overpay

  • Ask for an itemized quote and compare an independent shop with the Toyota dealer.
  • Get a second quote from another shop before you agree.
  • Ask if the thermostat and belts are worth replacing at the same time.
  • Ask which pump brand is used and the warranty length.
  • Have the leak or overheating confirmed before approving the job.

FAQ

How much does a Toyota Camry water pump replacement cost?

Estimates run about $500 to $1,400 in the US, parts and labor included. Your exact quote depends on year, engine and area.

What are signs of a bad Camry water pump?

Look for a coolant leak near the front of the engine, a rising temperature gauge or a whining noise. Stop driving if the engine overheats.

Does the Camry water pump need replacing often?

Not usually, but a pump can leak or wear out with age, and cheaper replacement pumps may wear out sooner.

Should I replace other parts with the pump?

Many shops suggest replacing coolant and checking the thermostat and belts at the same time, since the labor overlaps.
