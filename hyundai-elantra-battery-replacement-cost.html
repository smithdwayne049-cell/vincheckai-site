Hyundai Elantra battery replacement cost

Replacing the battery in a Hyundai Elantra in the United States usually costs about $290 to $490 installed. One estimate averages $289, and Kelley Blue Book ranges for several model years run from about $294 to $489. A bare battery is much cheaper than a full installation at a shop.

Estimates for United States, November 2026. Real prices depend on the car, the region and the shop.

What changes the price

  • Model year and battery size.
  • Standard or AGM battery.
  • Shop type: retailer, independent or dealer.
  • Whether labor is included.
  • Warranty length.
  • Local labor rates.

How not to overpay

  • Compare retailer installation with a shop quote.
  • Ask for an itemized quote that lists parts and labor separately.
  • Ask how long the warranty is.
  • Have the battery tested before replacing it.
  • Check that the quote matches your Elantra's battery size.

FAQ

How much does an Elantra battery replacement cost?

Most owners pay about $290 to $490 installed in the US. Prices depend on the year, battery type and shop.

How much is just the battery for an Elantra?

A standard car battery generally costs about $45 to $250 depending on quality and size, before any labor.

How long does an Elantra battery last?

Many batteries last around 3 to 5 years, depending on climate and use.

Is the Elantra Hybrid battery different?

The Hybrid also has a regular 12-volt battery, and its high-voltage pack is a separate, far costlier part.
