Privacy Policy for Vin AI

Effective Date: November 12, 2026

Vin AI (“we”, “our”, or “us”) is a VIN decoder and car report app for Android. This policy explains what data the app collects, why, who processes it and how you can delete it.

Information We Collect

  • Account data: your email address, and your name if you sign in with Google, to create and secure your account. You can also use the app as a guest without an account.
  • VINs: the VIN you type or scan. The camera image is read on your phone and is not uploaded.
  • History and purchases: if you have an account, your VIN and Compare history, the reports you paid for in the last 5 days and your token (credit) balance are saved to it, so they come back after reinstalling the app. As a guest, your history stays on your device.
  • AI assistant: the questions you ask and any photo you attach are sent to our server and to OpenAI to write the answer. We count how many messages you send (for the daily limit); we do not store your questions or photos.
  • Analytics: paywall steps, purchase results, the app version and your country (two-letter code), linked to a random install ID that is not linked to your name, email or VIN.
  • Welcome survey (optional): when you first open the app you can answer a short survey: why you are checking a car, when you plan to buy, your role (buyer, seller, dealer, …), whether you tried other VIN apps and where you heard about us. The answers are saved with the same random install ID and your country. You can skip the survey.

How We Use Your Information

  • To decode VINs, build car reports and comparisons and answer your questions
  • To confirm purchases and keep your paid reports and tokens
  • To understand how the app is used and improve it (analytics and the welcome survey)
  • To prevent abuse and keep the service secure

We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

Third-Party Services

These services process data on our behalf, only as needed to run the app:

  • Supabase (servers in Singapore): accounts, database and server functions
  • OpenAI: writing the car analysis and assistant answers
  • NHTSA (public U.S. vehicle database) and CarAPI.dev: vehicle data, recalls, complaints and safety ratings for a VIN
  • fueleconomy.gov (U.S. EPA): fuel economy for the decoded make and model
  • Google: Google Sign-In and Google Play billing
  • RevenueCat: confirming in-app purchases

Payments are handled by Google Play. We never see your card details.

Data Retention

  • Decoded vehicle data is kept on our server for up to 90 days so repeated checks are faster. It is linked to the VIN, not to you.
  • Account data, history, purchases and tokens are kept while your account exists and are deleted together with it.
  • Analytics and survey answers are not linked to your account and are kept to understand how the app is used.

Data Security

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access to the database is restricted to the app's own server functions.

Your Rights and Account Deletion

You can delete your account at any time in the app: Settings → Delete account. You can also write to us to access, correct or delete your data; we will answer within a reasonable time.

Children’s Privacy

Vin AI is not intended for children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes are published on this page with a new effective date.
