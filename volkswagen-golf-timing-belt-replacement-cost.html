Volkswagen Golf timing belt replacement cost

Replacing the timing belt on a Volkswagen Golf in the United States commonly costs about $420 to $1,400, parts and labor included. Estimates vary a lot by engine, and a diesel Golf SportWagen was estimated near $1,900 to $3,000. Many newer Golf engines use a chain instead of a belt.

Estimates for United States, November 2026. Real prices depend on the car, the region and the shop.

What changes the price

  • Engine: gasoline or diesel.
  • Whether the water pump and tensioner are replaced at the same time.
  • Labor, since many parts must come off to reach the belt.
  • Dealer or independent shop.
  • Model year, because some Golf engines use a timing chain.
  • Parts brand.

How not to overpay

  • Check your owner's manual: some Golfs have a chain and need no belt.
  • Ask to replace the tensioner, pulleys and water pump together.
  • Ask for an itemized quote that lists parts and labor separately.
  • Get a second quote from another shop before you agree.
  • Ask for the interval your engine really needs.

FAQ

How much does a Golf timing belt replacement cost?

In the US it usually costs about $420 to $1,400. Diesel models can cost more, near $1,900 to $3,000 in one estimate.

How often should a Golf timing belt be replaced?

Intervals vary by engine, commonly between 60,000 and 100,000 miles. Check the owner's manual for your engine.

Does my Golf have a timing belt or a chain?

Some Golf engines use a belt and others a chain. A chain tensioner job is different and was estimated at about $1,300 to $1,700 for a 2018 Golf.

Should I replace the water pump with the belt?

If it is driven by the belt, yes. The labor overlaps, and one source says doing it in the same job adds only about $100.
