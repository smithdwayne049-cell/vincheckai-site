Honda Civic clutch replacement cost

Replacing the clutch on a Honda Civic in the United States usually costs about $1,000 to $2,200, parts and labor included. One guide puts a typical Civic job at $1,000 to $1,500, and a similar Honda model was estimated near $1,800 to $2,200. The OEM clutch kit alone is about $600 to $700.

Estimates for United States, November 2026. Real prices depend on the car, the region and the shop.

What changes the price

  • Model year and engine, including Si models.
  • OEM or aftermarket clutch kit.
  • Whether the flywheel is resurfaced or replaced.
  • Shop labor rates.
  • Dealer or independent shop.
  • Extra parts such as the release bearing and fluid.

How not to overpay

  • Ask whether the flywheel is resurfaced or replaced and what it costs.
  • Ask for an itemized quote that lists parts and labor separately.
  • Get a second quote from another shop before you agree.
  • Ask if the kit includes the release bearing.
  • Ask about OEM kit and aftermarket kit prices.

FAQ

How much does a Honda Civic clutch replacement cost?

Most jobs cost about $1,000 to $2,200 in the US, parts and labor included. Many Civics fall near $1,000 to $1,500.

How much is a Honda Civic clutch kit alone?

One guide says the OEM clutch kit usually costs about $600 to $700 before labor.

Why is labor so much of a clutch job?

The transmission has to be removed to get to the clutch, so labor is often half or more of the total.

Is a simple clutch adjustment cheaper?

RepairPal lists a clutch adjustment for a Civic at about $58 to $85, but that is not a replacement.
