Toyota Corolla brake pad replacement cost

Replacing brake pads on a Toyota Corolla in the United States usually costs about $100 to $350 per axle, parts and labor included. Independent shops are often at the lower end, and rotors add more if they also need replacing. Dealers usually charge more.

Total, pads, one axle$100 – $350
Part of the jobLowHigh
Total, pads, one axle$100$350

Estimates for United States, November 2026. Real prices depend on the car, the region and the shop.

What changes the price

  • Front or rear axle.
  • Whether the rotors are replaced as well.
  • Pad type and brand.
  • Dealer or independent shop.
  • Model year and trim, including hybrid.
  • Local labor rates.

How not to overpay

  • Compare an independent shop with the Toyota dealer.
  • Ask for an itemized quote that lists parts and labor separately.
  • Ask if the rotors really need replacing.
  • Ask which pad brand is used.
  • Check pad thickness yourself before a shop says they are worn.

FAQ

How much does it cost to replace brake pads on a Toyota Corolla?

About $100 to $350 per axle in the US for pads, parts and labor included. Estimates vary by year, shop and area.

How often do Corolla brake pads need replacing?

It depends on how you drive. Squealing or grinding are signs it is time.

Do I need new rotors with new pads?

Not always. Rotors only need replacing if they are worn thin, warped or damaged.

Is the Corolla Hybrid brake job more expensive?

Estimates for the Hybrid are in a similar range.
